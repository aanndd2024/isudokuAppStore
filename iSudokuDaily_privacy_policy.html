Privacy Policy
Last Updated: Oct 25, 2026

iSudokuDaily is a simple offline Sudoku puzzle game. We respect your privacy.

Data Collection

iSudokuDaily does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
The game works entirely offline on your device. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

What we DON'T collect

  • No name, email, or contact info
  • No location data
  • No gameplay analytics sent to servers
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No cookies or tracking

Local Storage

Your game progress, settings (sounds, haptics), best times, and stars are stored only locally on your device using iOS standard storage. This data never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

iSudokuDaily is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children.
