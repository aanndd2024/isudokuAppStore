How to Play

  • Tap New Game > Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert
  • Tap a cell, then a number to fill
  • Use Notes toggle for pencil marks, Hint if stuck
  • 3 mistake limit - try to get 3 stars!

Settings

Tap gear icon to toggle Sound Effects, Music, Haptics, Key Tap sounds.

FAQ

Does it work offline? Yes, 100% offline. No account needed.

Is my progress saved? Yes, best times and progress are saved locally on your device.

App crashing? Try delete and reinstall, or restart device. Your best times will reset as they are local.
